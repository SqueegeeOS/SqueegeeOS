# HOMEATLAS LIVE — interactive homepage prototype

Isolated preview route: `/atlas-glass`. The production homepage remains
untouched. The route stays out of public navigation and search indexing while
the concept is reviewed.

## Concept

The page introduces SqueegeeKing as a modern home-care membership rather than
a one-time cleaning appointment. A clear campaign house anchors the first
view. Ambient sunlight, moving reflection, property-memory signals, staggered
cards, and pointer depth keep the scene alive without hiding the message.

Four real interface controls introduce the HomeAtlas promise immediately:

- Next Visit
- Photo Proof
- Service History
- Home Care Plan

The second scene turns those cards into an interactive portal preview using
sample data only.

## Design rules

- No fog, drawing task, or required interaction before the message is visible.
- All type, interface cards, navigation, and calls to action are real DOM.
- The campaign house is atmosphere, not a fabricated customer testimonial.
- Motion reinforces property memory through light, scanning, signals, orbit,
  and module transitions.
- Mouse, touch, keyboard, and reduced-motion visitors receive the full story.
- No audio and no production customer information.

## Before production integration

- Review on a physical phone and desktop.
- Choose whether this becomes one homepage section or a campaign landing page.
- Keep the route isolated until its real homepage placement is approved.
