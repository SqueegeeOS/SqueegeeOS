## 1. The relationship

This Master Service Agreement establishes the durable terms governing home-care services provided by the Service Provider to the Customer. Each property, service scope, cadence, first-visit rate, continuing rate, and first-year total must appear in a separate Property Service & Quote Agreement accepted by both parties.

The Service Provider is not required to perform work that does not appear in an accepted Property Service & Quote Agreement, a customer-approved add-on, or another written service order.

## 2. Property access and customer responsibilities

The Customer will provide safe and lawful access to agreed work areas, disclose known hazards, secure pets, and make reasonable preparations described in the property-specific agreement. The Service Provider may pause, reschedule, or decline unsafe work. Weather, smoke, access restrictions, equipment conditions, and other circumstances outside reasonable control may require rescheduling.

## 3. Care standard and re-service process

The Service Provider will perform accepted services in a professional manner consistent with the defined scope. The Customer should report a service concern promptly through the private HomeAtlas portal, the notice email above, or the published company phone. The final agreement should state the attorney-approved re-service window and the remedy that applies.

## 4. Scheduling, personnel, and service partners

Service dates are scheduling targets unless the property-specific agreement expressly guarantees a date. The Service Provider may use trained employees or insured service partners subject to the same accepted scope and care standard. Counsel must approve any subcontracting, licensing, or insurance representation.

## 5. Payment framework

No price is created by this MSA alone. The applicable Property Service & Quote Agreement controls the first-visit rate, continuing per-visit rate, estimated first-year total, included services, optional services, taxes, credits, and billing cadence.

If the Customer separately accepts a standing payment authorization, the Customer may save a payment method through Stripe’s hosted page. HomeAtlas and the Service Provider do not receive or store the full card number. A later rate or material term change is prospective only and follows the notice and cancellation process stated in the property-specific agreement.

## 6. Communications and electronic records

The parties consent to electronic records and signatures for this transaction. Transactional notices may be delivered to the email or phone supplied by the Customer. Marketing texts require separate consent and are not a condition of purchasing services. The signed documents, DocuSign completion certificate, property scope snapshot, and payment-provider references may be retained as the transaction record.

## 7. Cancellation and termination

The Property Service & Quote Agreement controls property-specific cancellation terms, any initial-term enrollment-savings reimbursement, renewal, and any applicable California home-solicitation cancellation notice. A statutory cancellation right controls over a conflicting contractual term.

The Customer may submit a written request through the private HomeAtlas portal under **Member care → Request membership cancellation** or email `{{legal_notice_email}}`. Counsel must approve when cancellation becomes effective, how already-initiated charges and scheduled services are handled, and any right of the Service Provider to suspend or terminate service.

## 8. Risk allocation — counsel to supply

California counsel must supply or approve all warranty disclaimers, limitation-of-liability terms, indemnity language, insurance statements, claim deadlines, dispute-resolution procedure, venue, governing-law language, prevailing-party provisions, and any consumer-law carve-outs. HomeAtlas must not invent or auto-fill these terms.

## 9. Order of precedence

If documents conflict, the accepted Property Service & Quote Agreement controls scope, property details, cadence, rates, add-ons, cancellation disclosures, renewal disclosures, and other deal-specific terms. This MSA controls the remaining relationship terms. A later signed writing controls only to the extent it expressly changes an earlier term.

## 10. Notices, assignment, severability, and entire agreement — counsel review

Counsel must confirm the permitted notice methods, assignment restrictions, severability language, waiver standard, amendment method, counterparts, survival terms, and the complete integration clause.

## 11. Acceptance

Customer acknowledges receiving this MSA together with the separate Property Service & Quote Agreement and any applicable Notice of Cancellation. Customer has had the opportunity to review both documents before signing electronically through DocuSign.
