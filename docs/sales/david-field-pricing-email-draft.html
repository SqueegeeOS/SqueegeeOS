Build the rhythm
around the home.

David, start with the service the homeowner needs, then build a recurring care plan around it. Exterior windows might be quarterly while interior glass is once or twice a year; screens and solar can have their own frequency too. The goal is useful, ongoing care—not a rigid package that promises work they do not want.

Window-cleaning starting rate · per visit

Quarterly · 4 visits/year: home square footage × $0.10

Three visits/year: home square footage × $0.115

Twice/year: home square footage × $0.125

Start with the exterior base on each exterior visit. Add $50 on each visit that includes screens, $100 on each visit that includes interior cleaning, and $100 per window visit for a two-story home. Adjust an add-on when the real scope calls for it—for example, $75 for unusually extensive screens.

A 2,000-square-foot quarterly example

One possible plan—not the only plan.

Complete visit · example$350

If interior and screens are done once a year, the other three quarterly visits maintain the exterior at $200 each. If the homeowner wants screens cleaned every visit, those three visits are $250 each. This complete clean repeats annually in this example; another homeowner could choose a different mix.

Two clear quarterly choices

Essential · $950/year: one $350 complete visit, then three $200 exterior visits.

Plus · $1,100/year: one $350 complete visit, then three $250 exterior-and-screen visits.

Flexible example: four $200 exterior visits + two $100 interior visits + one $50 screen cleaning = $1,050/year. These are service values across scheduled visits, not an upfront charge or a monthly payment promise.

Solar panels · per panel, per visit

Quarterly $7  ·  Three visits/year $8  ·  Twice/year $9  ·  One-time $11

Solar can stand alone or join a window plan. Its visit frequency does not have to match the windows. Count the panels and show the homeowner exactly what happens on each visit.

How to say it at the door

“We can keep your exterior windows on a quarterly rhythm, then choose how often you want the interior, screens, and solar done. I’ll show you the price for each kind of visit, so your plan fits your home rather than forcing everything into one package.”

Quote the real home, not just the example. Show which services happen on each visit and the corresponding price. For unusual access, heavy buildup, or scope that does not fit these starting rates, take notes and bring Noah or Dasan in before promising a final price.
